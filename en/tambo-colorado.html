Tambo Colorado Inca Ruins Tour

Visit the best-preserved administrative Inca adobe complex on the Peruvian coast, complete with its original colors.

Adventure Details

Tambo Colorado (also known as Pucallacta) was a military and administrative outpost built under the reign of Inca Emperor Pachacutec in the 15th century.

Located 45 minutes up the valley from Pisco, this archaeological site is unique because its dry climate preserved the red, yellow, and white paintings on the adobe walls.

An exceptional cultural tour for history enthusiasts, providing a peaceful walk far away from overcrowded tourist hotspots.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does the Tambo Colorado tour cost?

The tour costs S/ 80 per person and is one of the few that includes everything: round-trip air-conditioned transport, an archaeology-specialized guide, and the entrance ticket to the complex and its site museum. Only lunch is on you.

Why is it called Tambo Colorado?

The name means 'Red Outpost', referring to the deep red mud paint coating the structures. Tambos were roadside rest and supply hubs along the Inca road network (Qhapaq Ñan).

Is it a difficult walk?

Not at all. The tour takes place on flat dirt paths inside the ruins, making it a very easy and educational walking tour for families.

What should I bring?

The site is located in a warm, dry valley. Bring sun protection, a hat, sunglasses, bottled water, and comfortable walking shoes.

Is it worth it if I am already visiting Cusco?

Yes: Tambo Colorado is unique because it preserves the original wall paint of its structures, something you will hardly see at the stone sites of Cusco. It is also crowd-free and combines perfectly with the Paracas tours.
