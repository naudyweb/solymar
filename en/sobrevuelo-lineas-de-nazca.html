Nazca Lines Flight from Pisco Airport

Admire the mysterious UNESCO World Heritage geoglyphs from the air, flying directly from Pisco Airport next to Paracas.

Adventure Details

The Nazca Lines are 1,500-year-old geoglyphs declared a UNESCO World Heritage Site. Flying from Pisco Airport lets you see them without the 7+ hour drive it takes to reach Nazca by land.

Aboard a Cessna Grand Caravan with a guaranteed window seat for every passenger, you will fly over the Ica and Ocucaje valleys to the Nazca plateau, where the pilot banks the plane to both sides over each figure.

You will spot the Hummingbird, the Monkey, the Spider, the Astronaut, the Condor, the Whale, the Hands, and the Tree, among more than 12 figures etched by the Nazca civilization into the desert floor.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does the Nazca Lines flight from Pisco cost?

The flight costs USD 260 per person and includes the 1h 40m flight, hotel transfers between Paracas and the airport, and an official flight certificate. The TUUA airport tax (approx. S/ 30 / USD 10, cash) is paid separately. Clear total price before you book.

How long does the whole experience take?

The flight itself lasts about 1 hour 40 minutes. Including transfers from Paracas and airport check-in, set aside 3 to 4 hours of your morning in total.

Is an original passport required?

Yes, to board the plane you must present your physical original passport (or National ID for Peruvian citizens). Copies or photos on your phone are not accepted by airport security.

Are the aircraft safe?

Absolutely. We partner exclusively with airlines licensed and supervised by the DGAC (Peruvian Civil Aviation Authority), flying Cessna aircraft under strict maintenance programs with experienced pilots.

Is there a weight limit?

Yes, for safety and balance you must declare your exact weight at booking. Passengers over 95 kg (210 lbs) may be required to pay for an extra seat.

Will I get airsick during the flight?

The plane makes gentle banking turns over each figure, and sensitive travelers may feel queasy. We recommend a light breakfast and, if you are prone to motion sickness, a pill 30 minutes before the flight.

Why are flights only in the morning?

Between 8:00 AM and noon, visibility over the plateau is at its best and there is less thermal turbulence. In the afternoon, coastal wind and fog can force rescheduling.
