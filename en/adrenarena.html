Adrenarena Paracas: Buggies & Sandboarding

Enjoy the ultimate sand dune adventure inside an exclusive private desert park in Paracas.

Adventure Details

Adrenarena is a private adventure park located just outside Paracas, designed exclusively for dune buggies and sandboarding activities.

With dunes towering up to 150 meters, it is the perfect playground to ride high-powered off-road buggies and slide down sandy slopes.

Being a private facility, we ensure controlled and safe routes away from the crowded public tracks of the region.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does Adrenarena cost?

The experience costs S/ 150 per person and includes entrance to the private park, the tubular buggy ride with a specialized driver, sandboards, and dune guide assistance. Transport from Paracas is available on request.

Is it suitable for children?

Yes, we adjust the speed based on passenger preference. We offer calm family rides as well as high-adrenaline circuits for thrill-seekers.

What is the difference between this and Huacachina?

Adrenarena is in Paracas (15 minutes from the hotels) and is a private, monitored park: exclusive trails, no crossings with other vehicles, and no need to travel 1 hour 20 minutes to Ica.

What should I bring?

Comfortable sportswear, closed athletic shoes, sunblock, and sunglasses to protect your eyes from blowing sand. Cameras and phones must be well secured inside the buggy.

What is the best time to go?

The afternoon slots from 3:30 PM are the favorites: the sand no longer burns for sandboarding and the circuit ends with the golden sunset light over the dunes.
