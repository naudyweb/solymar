Pisco Route Tour: Wineries & Vineyards of Ica

Visit historic and artisanal wineries in Peru's premier Pisco valley, with guided tastings at every stop.

Adventure Details

Pisco is Peru's national spirit and the sunny Ica valley is its historical cradle: Pisco grapes have been grown here since the 16th century. On the Pisco Route you will discover this heritage guided by local producers.

We visit a combination of renowned industrial wineries and family-run artisanal bodegas that still use grape-stomping presses, colonial-era stills, and clay jars (botijas), with the whole process explained from vine to glass.

At each stop you will enjoy guided tastings of different Pisco varieties (Quebranta, Italia, Acholado), plus sweet wines, cachina, and traditional mistelas from Ica.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does the Pisco Route tour cost?

The tour costs S/ 80 per person and includes round-trip transport from Paracas or Ica, a local guide, entrance to all wineries, and the tastings at every stop. Only lunch and any bottles you purchase are extra.

Which wineries do we visit?

The circuit combines renowned industrial wineries of the Ica valley with family-run artisanal bodegas that preserve colonial presses and stills. The selection may vary by day and by harvest season.

Can minors join the tour?

Yes, children and teenagers are welcome to learn about the history and explore the vineyards. However, the alcohol tastings are strictly for adults aged 18 and older.

Is lunch included in the price?

No, the tour stops at a countryside winery restaurant where you can try Ica specialties like carapulcra with sopa seca. Meals are paid directly at the restaurant.

Can I buy wines and Piscos directly at the wineries?

Yes, all wineries have shops selling at cellar-door prices. It is the best chance to take home a pure Pisco or a Pisco cream liqueur.

When is the best time for the Pisco Route?

The tour runs great all year, but February–March coincides with the grape harvest (vendimia): you will see the picking and grape-stomping live, and in March, Ica celebrates its International Harvest Festival.
