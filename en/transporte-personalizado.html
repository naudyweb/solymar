Custom Private Transport to and from Paracas

Travel door to door between Lima, Paracas, Ica or Nazca in a private vehicle, and turn the journey into a tour with stops tailored to you.

Adventure Details

Our custom private transport service connects Paracas with Lima (including Jorge Chávez Airport), Ica, Huacachina, Nazca, or any city along the southern route, in comfortable private vehicles with a professional driver.

The difference is the journey itself: instead of a direct drive, you can add tours and stops en route that adapt to your itinerary — Pisco Route wineries, the Inca site of Tambo Colorado, the Huacachina Oasis, or the Paracas tours before departing.

You choose the departure time, the stops, and the pace; we handle the door-to-door logistics, with your luggage always with you — no bus connections, no waiting at terminals.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does the private transport cost?

The rate is per vehicle (not per person) and depends on the route, the stops you want to add, and the vehicle type. Send us your itinerary on WhatsApp and we will reply within minutes with a fixed quote — no hidden charges, no surprises.

Which routes do you cover?

The most requested are Lima ⇄ Paracas (including Jorge Chávez Airport), Paracas ⇄ Ica / Huacachina, and Paracas ⇄ Nazca. We also cover Chincha, Cañete, Lunahuaná, and any point along the Pan-American South highway.

What tours can I add along the way?

It depends on your route: towards Ica you can stop at the Pisco Route wineries or at Huacachina for buggies and sandboarding; up the valley, at Tambo Colorado; and before leaving Paracas, the Ballestas Islands or the National Reserve. We build the itinerary with you over WhatsApp.

How many people can travel?

We have cars for small groups and minivans for larger groups or bulky luggage. Tell us how many are traveling and we will assign the right vehicle — always for your group's exclusive use.

How far in advance should I book?

We recommend booking 24 to 48 hours ahead to guarantee availability. During high season (Easter, national holidays, long weekends), it is best to book several days in advance.

Is my luggage safe during the trip?

Yes. The vehicle is exclusive to your group and your luggage travels with you in the trunk for the entire journey, including tour stops — you never have to leave it at a hotel or storage.
